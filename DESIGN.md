# Tendo — art as a journey

Original art direction: cobalt virtual gallery, warm orange signals, off-white archive, oversized Syne lettering and staggered photographic masonry. Real public art images act as the material; no decorative generated product claims.

The gallery is an original Three.js implementation: framed textured image planes at different depths, a scrolling camera, gentle lateral perspective, a restrained ground grid, drag movement, reduced-motion mode and offscreen suspension. The visual archive is separately available as ordinary linked images for accessibility and no-WebGL fallback.

Inspiration: https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/03/09/building-a-scroll-reactive-3d-gallery-with-three-js-velocity-and-mood-based-backgrounds/ — scrolling through a spatial image collection. No tutorial code copied. https://www.awwwards.com/websites/art/ — artwork-first portfolio composition. The result uses separate archive, individual work, commission, studio and workspace pages rather than a one-page brand template.

Business workflow: save image references, supply useful specifications, export a commission brief, optionally demonstrate the creator inbox. No direct studio messaging or order acceptance.
